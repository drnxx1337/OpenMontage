File facts: both are variable fonts, `fvar` `wght` 300→700 (default 300, hence the internal name "Space Grotesk Light"), 1 000 upem, 291 vs 429 glyphs.

**Range arithmetic** (declared ranges parsed from `localFonts.ts:31,34`): latin ends at U+00FF, latin-ext starts at U+0100 — complementary across the base block. They deliberately overlap on **7** codepoints (`U+0131`, `U+0152-0153`, `U+2020`, `U+0304`, `U+0308`, `U+0329`) — that overlap is inherited verbatim from Google's own stylesheet. It is harmless here because every one of the 11 codepoints present in *both* files (incl. combining marks U+0300/0301/0303/0304/0308/0309/0323) has **byte-identical outlines**: 11/11 identical, 0 different.
